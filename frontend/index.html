    Automated Processing Pipeline

    1. Multi-Format Text Extraction

    Extracts structured content from PDF, DOCX, and plaintext documents.

    Tesseract OCR engine automatically analyzes scanned PDFs and image-based resumes with image enhancement.

    3. NLP & 1,000+ Skill Taxonomy

    Detects technical skills, certifications, experience durations, and education degrees.
